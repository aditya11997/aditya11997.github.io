Research

Applied research in AI assurance.

Manuscript under review Trustworthy AI AI Agents Regulated Systems

NormTrace: Incremental Assurance Invalidation and Behavioral Revalidation for Lending Agents Under Dependency Change

NormTrace studies a practical assurance problem in AI decision-support systems: external dependencies can change even when the application code does not. The framework propagates dependency changes through typed lineage, identifies affected assurance claims and behavioral tests, executes targeted replay, and binds resulting evidence to an explicit assurance lifecycle.
